Campbell Dibb
Four directions

Four different takes on the same brief: promote Campbell's work as a barber, drive people to his Instagram, and credit Known without selling for them. Open each one, view it on your phone as well as a laptop, and pick the one that feels like him.

Dark / Brass

01. The Editorial

Lookbook, oversized type, marquee

Dark and confident, built like a fashion lookbook. His name huge at the top, cuts indexed as numbered looks. The safest strong choice.

Paper / Stamp

02. The Ledger

Typewriter, cut records, analogue

An old appointment book brought to life. Every photo mounted like a taped-in record with a figure number. Quiet, crafted, personal.

Print / Red

03. The Broadsheet LOCKED IN

Magazine masthead, cover story, issues

His brand as a music magazine front page. DIBB as the masthead, cuts written up like features, following him framed as subscribing. Loud and cultural. The chosen direction, the others below are kept as alternates.

Navy / Grid

04. The Blueprint

Technical drawing, callouts, spec table

Precision as the whole brand. Cuts and tools annotated like engineering schematics on a drafting grid. Nobody else in barbering looks like this.
